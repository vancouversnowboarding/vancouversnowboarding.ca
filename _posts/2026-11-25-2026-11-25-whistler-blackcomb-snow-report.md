Whistler side opened today after 27 cm of overnight snow. The 24-hour total was 40 cm, with a 91 cm base.

Warm temperatures left the bottomless powder heavy and hard. Groomed runs were in great condition, but off-piste conditions were the worst. The mountain was not very busy.

Overall rate: 3/5 ★★★☆☆
